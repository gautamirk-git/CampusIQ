# Georgia Tech ISyE Undergraduate Program

## Program Rankings & Overview

The H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering offers a Bachelor of Science in Industrial Engineering (BSIE) that has been "ranked the #1 program of its kind in the nation since 1991" according to U.S. News & World Reports.

## Degree Concentrations

Students can specialize in:
- Analytics & Data Science
- Artificial Intelligence/Operations Research for Decisions
- Economic & Financial Systems
- Operations Research
- Advanced Studies for Operations Research and Statistics
- Quality & Statistics
- Supply Chain Engineering
- General Industrial Engineering

## Pathway Programs

The school offers combined BSIE/MS pathways for:
- Analytics
- Statistics or Computational Science & Engineering
- Industrial Engineering, Operations Research, or Health Systems
- Quantitative and Computational Finance
- Supply Chain Engineering

Additional options include a Minor in Health Systems, Study Abroad programs, and a Research Option.

## Career Outcomes

The median starting salary for BSIE graduates is $74,000. Graduates pursue roles including Process Engineer, Quality Engineering, Systems Engineer, Consultant (various specializations), and graduate school.

Major employers include Apple, Google, Microsoft, McKinsey & Company, Deloitte, UPS, Coca-Cola, and Delta.

## Admissions

Students apply through Georgia Tech's Office of Undergraduate Admissions with no separate ISyE application required.

## Mission

Through innovative research, hands-on learning, and strong industry collaboration, ISyE is preparing leaders who solve complex problems and improve systems across the globe.
