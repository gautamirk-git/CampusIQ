# Georgia Tech College of Computing: Prospective Undergraduate Students

## Introduction

The College of Computing offers three undergraduate degree programs designed to prepare students for leadership in computing. Georgia Tech's program ranks 5th nationally according to the 2026 U.S. News and World Report rankings.

## Three Undergraduate Degree Programs

### B.S. Computer Science

This program prepares students with foundational knowledge in computer science principles combined with specialized skills. "Employers need graduates with both a solid foundation in the principles of computer science and specialized computing skills and backgrounds."

### B.S. Computational Media

A joint offering with the School of Literature, Media, and Communication and the School of Music, this program combines computing education with in-depth study of visual design, music technology, or media history.

### B.S. Computer Engineering

Jointly offered with the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, this program covers hardware, software, and computing principles applied to systems including smartphones, Internet of Things devices, streaming platforms, and cloud computing infrastructure.

## Beyond the Classroom

The College highlights opportunities in career services, undergraduate research, entrepreneurship, international programs, student organizations, and community engagement. Students can participate in programs like Vertically Integrated Projects (VIP) and access resources including mentoring and tutoring assistance.
