# Georgia Tech Scheller College of Business - Undergraduate Curriculum

## Bachelor of Science in Business Administration (BSBA)

### Open Doors to Your Career

The bachelor's degree program at Georgia Tech Scheller College of Business provides students with "the experience and education you need to take on what's next." The program allows students to complete all degree requirements, two internship semesters, and one semester abroad within four years.

### Admission and Structure

Scheller students are admitted directly into the business program, meaning they can start taking business courses right away. Unlike other Colleges which require two years prior to declaring a major, Scheller College Undergraduate Program allows a student to select a concentration by the end of their sophomore year.

## Key Program Components

### Concentrations
Students can utilize 18 credits of electives to pursue an in-depth concentration area that "give you a competitive advantage in your career." Students can choose between eight concentrations: Accounting, Finance, General Management, IT Management, Leadership and Organizational Change, Marketing, Operations and Supply Chain Management, and Strategy and Innovation.

### Student Certificates
The program offers 12 student certificates designed to "Enter the job market with the skills and knowledge needed to get your career on track" and serve as enhancements to the degree.

### Minors
Additional minors are available to complement the main area of study and provide "in-depth knowledge about an additional field," including a new FinTech minor building in-demand skills for careers in emerging industries.

## Curriculum Design

The curriculum is designed to fit in a semester abroad and two full internship experiences within four years. Students begin their educational journey taking core courses within Georgia Tech in English, Mathematics, Humanities, Social Science, Computer Science, Health, and Laboratory Science giving them a holistic foundation to their business education.
