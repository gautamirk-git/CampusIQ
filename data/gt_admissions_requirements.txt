## First-Year Admission at Georgia Tech

### Application Requirements

First-year students may apply for summer or fall terms only. A completed application requires:

- Non-refundable application fee
- High school transcript (or equivalent)
- Additional documentation for international applicants and homeschooled students

Georgia Tech accepts the "Common Application for first-year admission." Georgia Tech exclusively uses the Common App for first-year admission; applicants must create a Common App account before beginning their application.

### Standardized Test Scores

Applicants may self-report standardized test scores through the Common Application testing section or via a form in the Admission Portal after submission. The institution will not accept scores via email, fax, or postal mail. However, admitted students who choose to enroll must submit official scores from the testing agency.

The middle 50% SAT scores for admitted Georgia Tech students were 1370-1530, and the middle 50% for ACT scores is 30-34.

### Academic Profile

The average high school GPA of first-year, first-time Georgia Tech undergraduates in the 2024-2025 academic year was 4.14.

First-year applicants must share any dual enrollment work they completed or are attempting in high school, along with grades received in those classes, which may be included on the official high school transcript or listed through the Common App.

### Application Deadlines

Applicants bear responsibility for submitting all required materials by stated deadlines. "All elements must be received prior to the first-year application deadlines." Details are available at https://admission.gatech.edu/first-year/deadlines.

### Application Review

The Office of Undergraduate Admission reviews all completed applications received by deadline. First-year applicants to Georgia Tech are reviewed in a selective, holistic admission process.

### Colleges and Majors

Georgia Tech offers six colleges and 39 majors across its academic programs.
