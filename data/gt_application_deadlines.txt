# First-Year Application Plans and Deadlines

Georgia Tech employs the Common Application exclusively for first-year applicants, considering students for both Summer and Fall terms regardless of application plan.

## Complete Applications Include:

- Common Application form
- Transcript(s) showing 9-12 courses with 9-11 grades visible; deferred/Regular Decision applicants must submit updated transcripts with Fall senior grades
- Self-reported or official SAT/ACT scores
- Additional documents requested via admission portal

## 2026-2027 Deadline Comparison

| Item | Early Action 1 | Early Action 2 | Regular Decision |
|------|---|---|---|
| Opens | August 1 | August 1 | August 1 |
| Application Deadline | October 15 | November 2 | January 6 |
| Document Deadline | October 30 | November 16 | January 22 |
| Test Scores Deadline | November 16 | December 8 | January 22 |
| Decision Release | Early December | Late January | Mid-March |
| Deposit Deadline | May 1 | May 1 | May 1 |

## Key Notes:

All deadlines operate at "11:59 p.m. in your time zone." Late transcripts should be submitted as soon as possible. The document deadline applies to materials outside the Common Application, including portfolios and language proficiency interviews.

## Eligibility:

Early Action 1 serves Georgia high school students and Georgia residents. Early Action 2 accommodates non-Georgia and international applicants. Incomplete Early Action files may proceed through Regular Decision before closure.

## Scholarship Consideration:

Merit-based scholarship consideration requires completion through an Early Action plan meeting appropriate deadlines, with no separate application needed.
