# Georgia Tech Woodruff School Undergraduate Program

## Overview

The Woodruff School encompasses the School of Mechanical Engineering and the School of Nuclear & Radiological Engineering and Medical Physics. The school provides "enhanced facilities and laboratories to create the best learning environment for outstanding faculty and students."

## Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering

The BSME program offers an interdisciplinary curriculum with optional concentrations including:
- Automation and Robotics
- Automotive Engineering
- Design
- Manufacturing
- Mechanics of Materials
- Micro & Nano Engineering
- Nuclear Energy
- Thermal, Fluid and Energy Systems

Degree Designators:
- Co-Op Plan
- International Plan
- Research Option

Students may also participate in undergraduate research, study abroad, work abroad, internships, and the BS/MS program.

## Bachelor of Science in Nuclear & Radiological Engineering

This program focuses on nuclear energy production, radiation protection, and medical/industrial radiation applications. The optional degree designator is the Co-Op Plan.

## Educational Objectives (Mechanical Engineering)

Graduates will be recognized leaders, global collaborators, committed to professional growth, and entrepreneurially minded innovators with positive economic, social, and environmental impact.

## Educational Objectives (Nuclear & Radiological Engineering)

Graduates will positively contribute to the field while maintaining "the highest professional and ethical principles that include considerations of public health, safety and the environment" and engage in lifelong learning.

## Student Outcomes

Upon graduation, students will have:
- Ability to identify, formulate, and solve complex engineering problems
- Ability to apply engineering design considering public health, safety, and global factors
- Effective communication skills
- Understanding of ethical and professional responsibilities
- Effective teamwork capabilities
- Experimental and analytical skills
- Capacity for continuous learning
