Georgia Tech is a leading public research university committed to advancing technology and improving the human condition. Founded in 1885, the Institute combines rigorous academics with a culture of innovation, service, and global impact. More than 53,000 students from all 50 states and over 146 countries study here, preparing to shape industries and strengthen communities.

## Innovation and Outcomes

Georgia Tech is nationally recognized for innovation, research strength, and long-term value. The Institute is ranked No. 3 among Most Innovative Schools by U.S. News and World Report and is a global leader in translating research into real-world impact.

Georgia Tech Research Corporation ranks No. 38 worldwide for U.S. utility patents, reflecting the Institute's strength in discovery, invention, and commercialization.

Tech graduates consistently experience a strong return on investment. The Institute is ranked among the top public universities for return on investment by PayScale, WalletHub, and The Princeton Review.

## Academics

Tech offers a rigorous, interdisciplinary academic experience across seven Colleges spanning business, computing, design, engineering, liberal arts, lifetime learning, and the sciences.

Students engage in hands-on learning through research, project-based coursework, and real-world application across disciplines.

Academic rigor is balanced by a vibrant campus environment that includes more than 500 student organizations, campus traditions, and Division I athletics.

## Research

As a Carnegie-classified R1 institution, Georgia Tech is among the nation's top public research universities. Its research enterprise brings together faculty, students, industry partners, and government agencies to advance discovery and deliver solutions with national and global impact.

With more than $1.3 billion annually in research awards across all Colleges and the Georgia Tech Research Institute, Georgia Tech supports interdisciplinary work in areas such as health, sustainability, security, data and computing, and advanced technology.

## Atlanta and Our Statewide Impact

Georgia Tech is proud to serve the state of Georgia as a public research university rooted in Midtown Atlanta. Our location in the state's capital and economic center strengthens connections to Georgia's communities, industries, and public institutions.

Through education, research, and partnership, Georgia Tech supports Georgia's workforce and drives economic growth. As a public research institution, the Institute also helps address challenges facing the state.

From developing talent to advancing innovation that benefits communities across Georgia, the Institute's work reflects a deep commitment to the people and future of the state.

## History and Traditions

For more than a century, Georgia Tech has combined academic excellence with a spirit of Progress and Service. Institute traditions, student pride, and a forward-looking culture continue to shape the Georgia Tech experience today.

## Athletics

Georgia Tech is home to the Yellow Jackets and competes at the NCAA Division I level. Athletics are an important part of campus life, fostering school spirit, community, and alumni engagement.
