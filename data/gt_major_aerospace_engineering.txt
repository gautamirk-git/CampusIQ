# Georgia Tech Aerospace Engineering Undergraduate Program

## Overview

The Daniel Guggenheim School of Aerospace Engineering provides "one of the largest and most rigorous" undergraduate programs in the nation. Students develop expertise in aerospace engineering concepts and applications across science, industry, and government sectors. Georgia Tech is home to the nation's top-ranked public aerospace engineering undergraduate and graduate programs.

The 131 credit-hour Aerospace Engineering undergraduate curriculum (BSAE degree) is built on fundamental disciplines that help students understand the design and performance of aerospace vehicles and their integrated subsystems.

## Degree Offerings

Bachelor of Science in Aerospace Engineering (BSAE) is the primary degree. The school also offers a minor in aerospace engineering for students majoring in other disciplines.

## Program Components

### Curriculum
- Major and elective requirements for BSAE completion
- Specialty areas enabling students to gain expertise across aerospace industry sectors, through a choice of Options and a choice of air- or space-focused tracks
- Core aerospace engineering courses
- Options/elective courses for specialization
- Courses in complementary areas including humanities, social sciences, and wellness

### Program Options and Pathways
- BSAE with COOP option, where students alternate between industrial assignments and classroom studies until they complete three semesters of work
- An International Plan with specific language requirements and coursework related to history, global economy, international culture, and residential foreign experience
- A Research Option, requiring at least nine units of undergraduate research over at least two, preferably three, terms
- BS/MS Honors Program (earn both degrees in five years)

### Research Opportunities
Students can engage in research across eight multidisciplinary research areas to expand their professional and academic capacities.

### Additional Options
- Study abroad opportunities
- Capstone design competition
- Internships and co-ops
- Minor programs
- Certificates

## Support Resources

- Undergraduate academic advising
- Tutoring and academic support services
- Course planning tools and flow charts
- Five-year course schedules
- Hardware requirements guidance
- Mentors in Residence program connecting students with aerospace alumni

## Student Outcomes

The program develops key attributes of a future BSAE graduate through structured learning outcomes and objectives.
