# Georgia Tech Financial Aid and Scholarships

## Financial Aid Homepage — Mission Statement

The Office of Scholarships and Financial Aid (OSFA) assists students and families with applications and receipt of financial resources for educational expenses at Georgia Tech. They commit to providing excellent service, administering funds effectively and in compliance with regulations, exercising good stewardship of funds, and advocating for strategic financial support.

Approximately 71% of Georgia Tech's undergraduate population received grant aid that averaged $12,022, and 73% of students receive financial aid overall. Georgia Tech awards students more than $105 million in need and merit-based aid.

Georgia Tech is a no-loan school, meaning its financial aid packages offer students grants and scholarships in place of federal loans, making its financial aid more generous than most.

## Undergraduate Types of Aid

### Scholarships
Scholarships are considered gift aid (or free money) because they generally do not need to be paid back. They may be awarded by governments, colleges, and private organizations.

### Grants
Grants are a form of financial aid that also do not need to be paid back. These funding sources include federal and state governments, as well as Georgia Tech itself.

### Loans
Loans are borrowed money from a bank, government or lending institutions with the understanding that the money has to be paid back. Interest charges accompany loan repayment obligations.

### Work Study
The Federal Work-Study program serves undergraduates demonstrating financial need. Participants may use their earnings toward tuition and related educational expenses.

### Additional Options
The institution recognizes that some students may not qualify for need-based aid programs. Georgia Tech provides a variety of creative ways to finance a Georgia Tech degree for these individuals.

## Application Requirements

All US Citizen and Permanent Resident undergraduate students must submit the "Georgia Tech Application for Scholarships and Financial Aid" and the "Free Application for Federal Student Aid" (FAFSA), with a priority application deadline of January 31.
